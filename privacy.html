The Fart Whisperer — Privacy Policy

This page is real. It is the only legal document here that is.

The Fart Whisperer is a work of interactive satire. The Terms, the fictional Privacy Policy inside the app, the Bureau, the Director, and the reference database of four million emissions are all part of the fiction. This page is not. This page describes, plainly and accurately, what the app actually does with your information.

Audio

Recordings are analyzed on your device, in memory, at the moment you make them. Audio is never uploaded, never transmitted, never stored by us, and never even written to a file. When the analysis completes, the audio is gone. We could not listen to your recordings if we wanted to; nothing carries them off your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours to send anything to. Your reading history, streaks, and certification progress are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage, and can be erased at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Data we share

None. There is nothing to share.

Permissions

The app requests microphone access only when you press Record, and works without it — a Simulated Sample mode is offered if you decline. Vibration is used for gentle feedback during analysis.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
